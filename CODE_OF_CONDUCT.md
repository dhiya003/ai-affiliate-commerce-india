# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive
feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward
others. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention,
personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
Report incidents privately to the repository owner. All reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for reporter privacy.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly
represents the project.
